5. AI FORM CHECK: this alone could be a whole company. super complex
6. SOCIAL FEED: need content moderation, reporting, etc
7. VIDEO COACHING: need video infrastructure like zoom
8. REAL-TIME FEATURES: leaderboards, challenges need real-time updates
